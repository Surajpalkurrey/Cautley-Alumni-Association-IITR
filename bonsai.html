Pilkhan Bonsai

Ficus virens · White Fig

Pilkhan (Ficus virens), also called the white fig, is a large strangler fig of the Indian subcontinent and beyond. It takes well to bonsai: its leaves reduce nicely in a container, and the new growth often flushes red or bronze before turning deep green. Trained as a bonsai, it keeps the look of a mature tree in a small pot.

Family
Moraceae
Native to
India to Australasia
Type
